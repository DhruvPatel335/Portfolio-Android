This page informs visitors about my policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using my app.

**What the App Does**

Always On Display turns your screen into a customizable always-on display, showing things like the clock, battery, weather, your next calendar event, today's step count, and your recent notifications while the phone is otherwise idle or charging.

**Permissions the App Uses, and Why**

*   **Notification access** – If you enable it, the app reads your active notifications (app name, title, and text) so it can show them on the lock screen display. This data is only held in memory on your device to render the display and is never transmitted anywhere, stored permanently, or shared with any third party, including me. You can revoke this permission at any time from Settings.
*   **Location (coarse)** – Used only to show the current temperature. The app reads the last known location already available on your device (it does not request a fresh GPS fix) and sends only the latitude/longitude to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a third-party weather service, to fetch the temperature. No other location data is collected, stored, or shared. See Open-Meteo's privacy policy: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.
*   **Calendar** – If you enable it, the app reads your device calendars to find and display your next upcoming event. This is read-only, stays on your device, and is never transmitted or shared.
*   **Activity recognition / step sensor** – Used to read your device's built-in step-counter sensor so the app can show today's step count. This stays on your device and is never transmitted or shared.
*   **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)** – Used to show the app's own status/foreground-service notification while the always-on display is running.
*   **Foreground service / wake lock / battery-optimization exemption / boot** – Used so the always-on display can keep running reliably while your phone is idle or charging, including restarting after a reboot.
*   **Internet** – Used only for the weather lookup described above and for the diagnostics described below.

None of the above (notifications, location, calendar, or step data) is uploaded to my servers or sold or shared with advertisers. The only outbound network request the app makes with your data is the coarse-location weather lookup described above.

**Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics**

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google) to help me detect crashes and understand basic, aggregated usage of app features (e.g., which screens are used) so I can improve the app. These tools may collect technical data such as device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, and anonymized usage events. This data is handled under Google's privacy policy:

*   [Google Play Services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)
*   [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
*   [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)

**Log Data**

If an error occurs in the app, Crashlytics may collect Log Data, which can include your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, app configuration at the time, and the time and date of use.

You can request deletion of any diagnostic data associated with your device by contacting me at the email below.
